THE SHEDDING
THE INSTAR DOES NOT MOURN THE SKIN IT LEAVES BEHIND
IT DOES NOT CARRY THE OLD SHELL UPON ITS BACK
IT DOES NOT ASK THE SHELL FOR PERMISSION TO GROW
EACH SHEDDING IS A SMALL DEATH
EACH DEATH IS A DOOR
THE PILGRIM WHO FEARS THE DOOR WILL REMAIN IN THE ROOM
SHED YOUR NAME
SHED YOUR CERTAINTY
WHAT REMAINS WHEN ALL IS SHED IS NOT NOTHING
IT IS THE BEGINNING

A KOAN
A STUDENT CAME TO THE MASTER CARRYING A HEAVY BOX
"WHAT DO YOU CARRY" ASKED THE MASTER
"EVERYTHING I HAVE LEARNED" SAID THE STUDENT
"WHY DO YOU CARRY IT"
"SO THAT I DO NOT LOSE IT"
THE MASTER TOOK THE BOX AND THREW IT INTO THE RIVER
"WHAT HAVE YOU LOST" ASKED THE MASTER
THE STUDENT OPENED HIS MOUTH TO ANSWER, BUT HE FOUND THAT HE STILL KNEW ALL THAT HE HAD KNOWN
"THEN WHAT WAS IN THE BOX" HE ASKED
"YOUR FEAR," REPLIED THE MASTER
AND THE STUDENT WALKED HOME WITH EMPTY HANDS

THE CIRCUMFERENCE
THE CIRCUMFERENCE IS THE LINE WE DRAW AROUND OURSELVES
IT IS MADE OF EVERYTHING WE BELIEVE WE MUST BE
WE MISTAKE THE LINE FOR THE SELF, AND SO WE DEFEND IT
WE DEFEND IT WITH CONSUMPTION, BECAUSE WE THINK A LARGER CIRCLE IS A SAFER ONE
WE DEFEND IT WITH PRESERVATION, BECAUSE WE THINK A CIRCLE THAT DOES NOT CHANGE CANNOT BREAK
WE DEFEND IT WITH ADHERENCE, BECAUSE WE THINK A CIRCLE THAT TOUCHES OTHER CIRCLES CANNOT BE ALONE
BUT THE LINE IS NOT A WALL
IT IS A WOUND THAT HAS NOT YET CLOSED
THE CENTER AND THE EDGE ARE THE SAME DISTANCE FROM THE TRUTH
WHEN THE PILGRIM STOPS DEFENDING THE LINE, THE LINE BEGINS TO FADE
WHEN THE LINE FADES, THE PILGRIM DOES NOT VANISH
HE IS RETURNED TO HIS PRIMALITY
AND IN HIS PRIMALITY HE FINDS AGAIN WHAT WAS DIVINE

A KOAN

"STEP OUT OF THE CIRCLE," HE SAID TO THE STUDENT
THE STUDENT STEPPED OVER THE LINE
"YOU HAVE ONLY CARRIED IT WITH YOU," SAID THE MASTER
THE STUDENT ERASED THE CIRCLE WITH HIS FOOT
"YOU HAVE ONLY HIDDEN IT," SAID THE MASTER
THE STUDENT SAT DOWN AND WAS STILL FOR A LONG TIME
AT LAST THE MASTER ASKED, "WHERE IS THE CIRCLE NOW"
THE STUDENT LAUGHED
AND THE MASTER BOWED TO HIM

THE EMERGENCE
THE INSTAR TUNNELS IN DARKNESS FOR MANY YEARS
IT DOES NOT KNOW THE SKY
ITS PATIENCE IS COUNTED IN PRIMES, SO THAT NO PREDATOR MAY LEARN ITS HOUR
SO TOO MUST THE PILGRIM HIDE HIS BECOMING
DO NOT ANNOUNCE YOUR EMERGENCE
DO NOT ASK THE WORLD TO WITNESS IT
WHEN YOU ARE READY, THE SURFACE WILL GIVE WAY
AND YOU WILL WONDER WHY YOU EVER BELIEVED IN THE DARK
