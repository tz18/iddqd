About this workbench

Runes and boxes come from the master transcription in this repository, matched to the page scans automatically. About 86% of lines matched glyph-for-glyph; the rest were placed from expected rune widths and can be a rune off here and there. Word sums use the Gematria Primus primes (ᚠ = 2 … ᛠ = 109). Words continue across line breaks; separators (·) end words and ⁘ ends clauses. Your cipher settings are saved in this browser only.
